Crazy Legs is a popping and funk-style dance move built around ball-of-foot pivots and circular knee movement. I learned Crazy Legs from Poppin’ Pete himself. Start with those basic actions, then explore how they work together.

Watch the demonstration below, then use the breakdown to begin practicing.

## Watch the Crazy Legs demonstration

[Watch the Crazy Legs demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c2nyWLoGuNM)

Poppin’ Pete and Skeeter Rabbit of the Electric Boogaloos demonstrate Crazy Legs in this video from Ceech Hsu’s Old School Dictionary series. The clip shows several variations rather than one required sequence.

## Learn the basic action

### Pivot on the balls of your feet

Keep pressure on the balls of your feet and let your heels lift as needed. Your feet are free to pivot as your knees move. Let the feet turn with the knees rather than keeping your feet planted and forcing a twist.

### Let your knees trace circles

Start with one knee at a time. Explore a small clockwise circle, then a small counterclockwise circle, allowing your foot to pivot with the movement. Try each side slowly and keep the movement comfortable.

Once you can follow the circles clearly, try using both knees.

## Explore the variations

You can move both knees together, move your knees in opposite directions, or let one knee lead and the other follow. Try leading with the right, then with the left. One foot can slide while the other pivots.

Poppin’ Pete’s demonstration shows several variations. Use the ball-of-foot pivots and knee circles as your starting point, then explore how they work together. You do not need to follow one fixed sequence.

## Practice one variation at a time

Choose one variation and repeat it at a pace you can control. Keep checking that your feet are free to pivot as your knees circle. Add another variation once you can follow the first without rushing.

Record a short attempt with your whole body and both feet in view. Watch the relationship between your feet and knees, and choose one detail to work on before recording again.

## Questions about Crazy Legs

### Who created the Crazy Legs dance move?

[Poppin’ Pete of the Electric Boogaloos is credited with innovating Crazy Legs](https://danceconsortium.com/features/article/the-electric-boogaloo-founding-members/). The video above features Poppin’ Pete and Skeeter Rabbit demonstrating the move.

### Is this the same as the breaker Crazy Legs?

Richard “Crazy Legs” Colón is a [breaker associated with the Rock Steady Crew](https://linktr.ee/crazylegsbx). This guide focuses on the popping and funk-style move that shares his name.
