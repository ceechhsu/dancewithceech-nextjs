Sound and camera tips

Use the phone’s built-in speaker for this first test. Turn the media volume up enough to hear the count-in. Bluetooth timing has not been validated.

Your camera and microphone are recorded together. The microphone is never played back through the speaker. The preview is mirrored; the saved recording is not.

This is framing guidance, not automatic body or foot detection.
