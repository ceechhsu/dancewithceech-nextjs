How your score works

Your timing score is out of 100. When all 16 steps are measured, each step contributes up to 6.25 points (100 ÷ 16).

  • On beat: up to 67 ms early or late — full credit (6.25 points).
  • Early or late: more than 67 ms and up to 100 ms — 50% credit (3.125 points).
  • Very early or very late: more than 100 ms and up to 150 ms — 25% credit (1.5625 points).
  • Missed beat: more than 150 ms early or late — no credit (0 points).

For example, 15 on-beat steps and 1 missed beat gives 94/100 after rounding. We round only the overall score.

If a landing cannot be detected, it is marked “Needs review” in the breakdown, not counted as a missed beat. Your preview score averages only the measured steps, so the 6.25-point share applies only when all 16 are measured. This measures foot timing, not overall dance technique.

    About this first prototype

    Matching looks for at least four seconds of distinctive shared audio. Repeated phrases, heavy noise, different edits, and recording clock drift can prevent a reliable match. Confidence labels are heuristics, not probabilities. The player uses a fixed audio offset and corrects video drift; browser playback is not guaranteed frame-perfect. Step buttons follow your recording’s actual frame timestamps and seek the reference to the corresponding synchronized time.

    Uploaded copies stay on the server until you clear them. Legacy unsaved comparisons older than 24 hours are removed next time the server starts. Lesson original recordings and saved comparisons remain until explicitly cleared. Saved links require this server to be running. On the private website, sign in to open them. The demo uses original synthetic audio and animated figures; student movement is 0.24 seconds late on purpose.
