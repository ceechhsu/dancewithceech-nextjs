# Running Man Landing-Page Teaser Design

## Goal

Create a concise, conversion-focused video that lets a first-time visitor immediately see the Running Man and understand that the four-week program teaches the rhythm, balance, and coordination behind the move with personal feedback.

## Approved direction

Use Option A: clean kinetic captions. The footage remains the visual focus; captions animate in short phrase groups with restrained upward motion and Dance With Ceech colors. Playback will be click-to-play rather than autoplay.

## Source assets

- Video: `videos/running-man-demo.mp4` (17.33 seconds, 1280×720, H.264/AAC)
- Voiceover: `videos/running-man-voice-over.m4a` (14.53 seconds, AAC, stereo)
- Keep both source files untouched.

## Edit structure

1. Trim the source to approximately 16 seconds while preserving the clearest continuous demonstration of Ceech and Margarita. The extra second provides a clean end card without rushing the voiceover.
2. Keep the original music audible, ducked approximately 10–14 dB under the voiceover and allowed to breathe briefly between phrases.
3. Use the voiceover as the timing master:

   “This is the Running Man. In my four-week program, I’ll teach you the rhythm, balance, and coordination behind this move—step by step, with personal feedback—so you can dance it with confidence.”

4. Add clean kinetic captions, with emphasis on “rhythm,” “balance,” “coordination,” and “personal feedback.” Captions use short phrase groups, a maximum of two lines, and safe margins inside the 16:9 frame. Timing follows the voiceover, with the first caption appearing after the opening beat and the final phrase completing before the end card.
5. Cover the distracting upper-right picture-in-picture inset with a small branded `RUNNING MAN METHOD` label that does not obscure either dancer.
6. End with a readable one-second message: `Learn the Running Man — step by step`.

## Output and verification

- Render `running-man-teaser-option-a-preview.mp4` first, then `running-man-teaser-option-a-final.mp4` after approval.
- Match the source’s 16:9 format and use web-friendly H.264/AAC output. Target total duration is approximately 16 seconds: 14.5 seconds of voiceover-led footage followed by a one-second end card and brief transition padding.
- Verify duration, audio presence, caption readability, no visible flash at cuts, no audio pops, and that both dancers remain visible.
- Store all generated assets under `videos/edit/`; do not overwrite the source files.

## Out of scope

- No Bobby Brown footage or unlicensed third-party media.
- No edits to the landing page until the video itself is approved.
